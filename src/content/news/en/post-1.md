As the novel coronavirus spreads and affects many people's lives, we extend our sincere sympathy to everyone.

At our company, we are taking great care to prevent infection, both among our staff and among the customers who visit us.

**Disinfecting the office**
We have installed air purifiers and clean with high-concentration alcohol.

**Hand-washing, gargling and masks for staff**
We ask visiting customers to wear a mask and disinfect their hands.

**Strict temperature and health checks**
Staff with a temperature of 37.0°C or higher will not come to work and will stay at home for a set period.

**Keeping a distance during visits**
When you visit, we ask you to keep a suitable distance, and we take care in every interaction.

We apologise for any inconvenience and thank you for your understanding and cooperation. We currently plan to operate as usual, but this may change depending on the situation. If it does, we will report it on this website. We hope the situation ends as soon as possible.

We also accept online consultations. If you would like one, please contact us using the link below.
