Reiwa 3 has begun. We expect this to be a year in which we accept new ways of living and adopt new ways of doing business.

We keep asking ourselves: "What can we do now?" Across Oita Prefecture, centred on Oita City and Beppu, we hope to share useful information about property transactions and sales, as well as overseas property trends, widely with our local community.

We hope that normal life returns as soon as possible, that many smiles return, and that everyone stays healthy.
