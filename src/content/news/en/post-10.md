Since the start of 2021, we have received more enquiries from clients in the Tokyo area who want to move to Beppu. Until now, most enquiries came from older clients who wanted to live slowly in a home with a hot spring. This year, possibly because of COVID-19, we are also hearing from clients in their 40s and 50s.

These are the three most common questions, in order:

- **Top 3: Are homes with a hot spring hard to maintain?**
- **Top 2: What are typical prices for used homes in Beppu?**
- **Top 1: I have no local knowledge. Please tell me about the current situation.**

Searching for a property from far away is hard. Where we can help, we can support you on the ground, and we can also arrange online property viewings.

Please feel free to contact us.
