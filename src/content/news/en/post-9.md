Good morning! It is again the season for final tax returns, and the housing loan tax deduction is closely tied to a tax return when you buy a home.

Under this system, if you use a housing loan to build a new home, or buy or extend or renovate a used home, a fixed percentage of the loan balance at the end of each year is deducted from your income tax.

This year the rules take account of the effects of COVID-19. The eligibility criteria and application methods are published on the Ministry of Land, Infrastructure, Transport and Tourism website (updated 4 January 2021).
