We were interviewed for a feature in the health magazine "Yurari", published by Nagatomi Pharmacy Co., Ltd., in its "Kagayaki-bito" series, which features women in Oita who always shine.

We spoke about our wish to give encouragement to the women of Oita who are working hard. Our motto is "Do what you can, in the way you can," and we will keep working, as a husband and wife team, to connect people with property.
