Good morning! On 1 February, the weather in Beppu feels like the arrival of spring, and it is pleasant outside.

As you may know, this year's setsubun (the day marking the start of spring) falls tomorrow, 2 February. That is said to be the first time in 124 years. Out of curiosity we looked into it and found a practice said to bring fortune into the home when carried out on the risshun (the first day of spring). It is simple and anyone can do it, so we would like to share it with as many people as possible.

Let us catch a little of the good fortune of spring, which arrives just a little early.

**Try these on risshun: a practice to invite fortune into your home**

According to Japanese eastern divination such as kyusei kigaku (nine-star ki astrology) and shichu suimei (the four pillars of destiny), the year begins at risshun. In kyusei kigaku, 2021 is a year of the Six White Metal Star. It is a year that asks us to pay attention to our essential character, to the eye that sees what is genuine, and to showing respect and courtesy.

These are some actions you might take this risshun. You do not need to do all of them; try any that feel right.

- Ventilate, clean, and dispose of things you no longer need.
- Wear well-made clothing of good quality.
- Wear good-quality accessories (platinum, pearls and so on).
- Put effort into your base make-up.
- Eat from good-quality tableware.
- If you are in a year of misfortune, visit your local guardian shrine. (If you do not know which shrine is yours, the Association of Shinto Shrines can tell you.)

This year's risshun falls on 2 February for the first time in 124 years, one day earlier than in recent years. Let us catch the good fortune of spring's early arrival.
