The town written 日の出る町 (the town where the sun rises) is read Hiji-machi, or Hiji Town, in Oita Prefecture.

Properties in Hiji Town, in Hayami District, are appealing.

At a time when population decline is much discussed, Hiji Town's population is growing. The Toyooka, Yōkoku Station and Kawasaki areas have good public facilities, shopping and restaurants, and good commuting access south towards Oita and Beppu and north towards Kitsuki and Kunisaki. The area is also calm and quiet to live in. The rental apartment "ARISE" in the Kawasaki area is becoming popular with families raising children and with couples. There are also little-known restaurants and sightseeing spots, so the town is well worth a visit.
