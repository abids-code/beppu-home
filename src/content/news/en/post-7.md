We received a sale request for a used detached house with a hot spring in Taikanzan, Beppu in September, and today we completed its handover to the new owner.

Shortly after the property was published, we received many enquiries from inside and outside Oita Prefecture.

We are glad that a home that carried the feelings of the previous owner will now be cherished by its new owner, and that new life will be breathed into it.

We sincerely wish good fortune for everyone involved.
